# All HomeWx Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-31_

All HomeWx ("the app") is published by LanDen Labs. This policy explains what the app does with data.

## Data stored only on your device
Settings, your zip code or location choice, sensor history, weather history and error logs are stored locally on your tablet. You can export and import this data yourself from Settings; the export file goes where you choose to save it. We do not receive it.

## Location
The app asks for precise location permission only so Android will reveal the name of the Wi-Fi network you are connected to. It uses the name to show a clearer "no data" message when the network is down. The app does not read, store or transmit your GPS position. You can deny the permission and the app still works. Weather and river locations come from the zip code you enter.

## Data sent to third parties
To show you content, the app sends requests to the services below. Those requests necessarily include your IP address and, for weather and river data, the coordinates of your zip code. Each service has its own privacy policy.
- Weather: Open-Meteo, and other weather providers you choose
- Zip code lookup: zippopotam.us
- Rivers: USGS Water Data
- Radar, maps and drought: Iowa Environmental Mesonet, NASA GIBS, Esri ArcGIS, US Drought Monitor, NOAA ACIS
- News and article viewer: the RSS feeds and web pages you configure
- Optional, only if you add your own credentials: Govee, Google Nest (Smart Device Management), Wyze. Your credentials are used only to sign in to that service.

## Analytics and crash reports
The app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to learn which features are used and to fix crashes. These collect app usage events, crash stack traces, device model, OS version and an app-instance identifier. They do not collect your name, email or contact information. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13.

## Retention and deletion
Data on your device is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Analytics and crash data are retained by Firebase under its standard retention period.

## Changes
We will update this page when the policy changes and revise the date above.
