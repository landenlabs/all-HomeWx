## Full description (max 4000)

All HomeWx turns a wall- or shelf-mounted tablet into an always-on weather and home dashboard. Current conditions, forecasts, radar, river levels, news and your indoor sensors share one continuously running, swipeable screen.

TABLET ONLY
Built for large landscape screens (7" and up). It is not designed for phones.

WEATHER
• Current conditions with a live background image that follows the weather
• Hourly and daily forecast as cards or as graphs (temperature, wind, precipitation, pressure)
• Swipe back through recent history or forward through the forecast
• Weekly precipitation history for the last 12 weeks
• Choose between multiple weather data sources

RADAR AND MAPS
• Animated radar, clouds and infrared satellite on a light or dark map
• US Drought Monitor

RIVER GAUGES
• Water levels from nearby USGS gauges, found by zip code

INDOOR SENSORS
• Temperature and humidity from Govee sensors, with history graphs
• Smoothing and flat-range controls so small changes are not exaggerated

NEWS
• RSS news feeds with thumbnails and a built-in article viewer

MADE FOR ALWAYS-ON USE
• The ambient light sensor dims the screen at night and brightens it when the room lights come back, so it needs no manual on/off
• Adjustable brightness, night brightness and light threshold
• Runs a lightweight background service to keep data fresh, and restarts after a reboot
• Export and import your settings

Optional integrations (Govee, Google Nest, Wyze) need your own account or API key. The app works without them.

Weather data from Open-Meteo and other providers, river data from USGS, radar from Iowa Environmental Mesonet and NASA GIBS, drought data from the US Drought Monitor.
