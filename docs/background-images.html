Your Own Weather Backgrounds

Last updated: 2026-11-14

The weather panel in All HomeWx shows a photo behind the current conditions that matches the weather: clear, cloudy, rainy or windy. You can replace the built-in photos with your own by putting image files in a folder on the device and choosing that folder in the app.

Setting it up

  1. Copy your images into a folder on the device, for example Documents/all-HomeWx. Images in sub-folders are not read.
  2. In the app, open Settings > Display > Your background images and tap Choose folder…, then pick that folder.
  3. The line under the button shows how many images matched for each condition. If a file is missing from the count, check its name against the rules below.

To go back to the built-in photos, tap Clear. Adding or removing files later is picked up when you return from Settings.

File names

Each file name starts with wx_bg_ and the condition, may add an underscore and any text you like, and ends with a supported extension:

wx_bg_<condition>[_anything].<jpg|jpeg|png|webp>
ConditionShown whenExample names
clearSunny or clear sky wx_bg_clear.jpg, wx_bg_clear_002.jpg
cloudyPartly cloudy, overcast, fog and anything else not listed here wx_bg_cloudy_lake.webp
rainyAny rain, showers, thunderstorms or snow wx_bg_rainy_01.png
windyWind of 20 mph or more, when it is not raining or snowing wx_bg_windy_beach.jpeg
  • Upper or lower case doesn't matter: WX_BG_Clear_01.JPG works.
  • You can have as many images per condition as you like. The app starts at a random one, then shows the next one in name order each hour, wrapping back to the first. Number them (_001, _002, …) to control the order.
  • A condition with no matching file keeps using the built-in photo, so you can replace only the ones you want.
  • Files with any other name are ignored.

Image types

TypeExtensionsNotes
JPEG.jpg, .jpeg Best choice for photos. Quality 80-85% is plenty.
WebP.webp Smaller files than JPEG at the same quality.
PNG.png Works, but photo files are much larger. Use it for artwork or graphics.

Phone photos taken sideways are turned upright using their EXIF orientation tag. HEIC, GIF and other formats are not read; convert them to JPEG first.

Image size

The weather panel always fills half the screen: the left half in landscape and the top half in portrait. Its shape therefore flips when the device rotates. On a tablet it is taller than wide in landscape and wider than tall in portrait.

The image is scaled to fill the panel and the overflow is cropped equally from both sides (or top and bottom), so:

  • Keep the subject near the center of the image. The edges may be cut off.
  • An image at least as large as the panel stays sharp. A smaller image is stretched and looks soft.
  • If the device is used in both orientations, a square image as wide as the panel's longer side covers both without being stretched.
  • Keep the longest side at about 2048 pixels or less. Larger images look no better on screen, use more memory and take longer to load.
  • The weather text is drawn on top of the photo. Busy or very bright photos are harder to read over; use Settings > Display > Darken background image to dim them.

Recommended sizes

Panel sizes are in physical pixels and are approximate: status and navigation bars take a little of the screen. The app's own panel size is shown in the highlighted row when this page is opened from the app.

Tips

  • Copying files from a computer with adb: 
    adb shell mkdir /sdcard/Documents/all-HomeWx
adb push wx_bg_*.jpg /sdcard/Documents/all-HomeWx
  • If an image can't be read (deleted, or the folder access was revoked) the app skips it and shows the next one. If the whole folder becomes unreadable, Settings says so; choose the folder again.
  • The app only reads the folder you choose. See the privacy policy.
